The question · from the last meeting

"It works in this range, with this variability": can we say that and defend it?

The formula under test, frozen since run 6 and never refitted:
believed switch rate h′ = 1.453 · h0.736  ·  believed typo rate logit ε′ = −0.345 + 0.438 · logit ε
predicted probe log-odds = 0.215 + 1.433 · Bayes(h′, ε′), on 112 conflict-probe cells
What was weak before. The band h ≈ 0.001–0.045, ε ≈ 0.05–0.25 came from a yardstick picked after seeing the results (error ≤ 1.51× the model's own Bayes fit). It had no number for how much models vary.
What we did this round. Fixed the band, the pass criteria and the grading code first, and saved their hashes with timestamps (00:20 and 00:34 IST). Then trained 148 models at settings never used before and graded them as registered.
The formula has two parts: a believed switch rate that is a power law in the true switch rate, and a believed typo rate that is linear in logit space. We plug those into the exact Bayes rule to predict the probe log-odds. The problem with our last answer was that the band came from a yardstick we chose after looking. So this time everything was written down and hashed before training: the band is exactly the one on the follow-up slides, and the code that grades it was fixed too.
