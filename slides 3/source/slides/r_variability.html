Result 2 · the variability

The formula's error is a steady bias, about twice the seed noise

Seed noise ≈ 0.26 nats: how much two training runs of the same recipe differ. No formula can beat this.
Formula bias ≈ 0.59 nats: larger than the noise at 23 of 24 settings. Rerunning the training predicts a model about 1.8× better.
But the bias is reproducible: seeds agree on the believed switch rate to ±9%. The formula is stable, not noisy.
This is the variability part of Sir's question. Grey bars are how much two seeds of the same recipe differ: about a quarter of a nat. Orange bars are how far the formula is from the average model, with that seed noise removed: about 0.6 nats. So the formula is not as good as simply rerunning the training. But its miss is the same from seed to seed, so it is a stable, systematic description, which is what we will claim.
