The map shows the range as the dashed box. Squares are the settings from earlier rounds; circles are the 24 new ones, blue where the formula does as well as it does on its own fitting data, orange where it does worse. Across the 72 new models the formula, never refitted, explains a median 89 percent of the variation in the models' behaviour, with a typical error of 0.68 nats, and the models' believed switch rate lands within a factor of 0.8 to 1.7 of the formula's value for 90 percent of them. The five weaker settings sit at the two ends of the switch-rate range. The two at high h have fairly low typo rates, which fits the tilt of the band we found before. The three at low h we did not expect; our written expectation of where failures would be matched only one of the five.
