Four questions from the talk

1

Is the formula right, and is its derivation right? Derive it again.

2

Where exactly does the formula work?

3

Why does it work inside that region?

4

Why does it fail outside it?

The aim is to understand, not to fit more. 8 rounds of tests; each prediction was written down, with a time, before its result was read.

These are the four questions you asked at the end of the talk, and the rest of the deck follows them in order. Question 1 is about the theory: the Bayes boundary in our paper and the fitted law. Question 2 asks for the exact region where the law works, which we had not mapped: run 6 tested only seven new settings. Questions 3 and 4 ask for an explanation of both sides, and you were clear that the point is to understand the behaviour, not to keep adding fitted terms. We worked in rounds. In each round we wrote down a hypothesis and what result would prove it wrong, with a timestamp, then ran the GPU jobs, then gave each prediction a pass or fail. Eight rounds in all, about 110 new models plus probes on about 140 old ones.
