What we ran

The main tests in plain words

TestThe questionHow
1 · Conflict probesWhich copy does the model trust, versus the ideal?112 probe types per model, compared with exact Bayes
2 · Rule fittingWhich formula best describes its choices?Counter rules vs Bayes with free beliefs (h′, ε′), scored on held-out probes
3 · ControlsIs it an artifact of encoding, size or training settings?3 encodings, depth, MLPs, 2× width, 3× training, lr and batch, new seeds
4 · Slope surgeryIs the heads’ fading rate the cause?Rescale trained ALiBi slopes ×0.25 to ×4, measure again
5 · Inside the modelWhich heads make the decision, and how?Split the output by head; cut paths between layers
6 · AuditAre the code and the numbers right?Unit tests, retrains, null models, numbers recomputed
Six kinds of test. The probes measure behaviour. Rule fitting asks which formula explains it. Controls rule out the obvious artifacts. Slope surgery is the causal test. The mechanism work looks inside. The audit checks that everything we report is real.
